# Formula Concentration

Helps clinicians work out how to make up a bottle of powdered infant formula at a prescribed energy concentration, using the labels of formulas sold in Australia and New Zealand.

## Language

Units and formula properties are written lowercase in running text: recipe, scoop, grams per scoop, kcal per scoop, displacement, total powder. They take a capital only to begin a sentence, heading or field label, and kcal is lowercase even there.

### Prescription and result

**Concentration**:
The energy density of prepared formula, always expressed in kcal per 30 mL.
_Avoid_: Strength, calorie density, kcal/oz

**Target volume**:
The total volume of prepared formula the clinician wants to make.
_Avoid_: Desired volume, bottle size

**Target concentration**:
The Concentration the clinician wants the prepared formula to have.
_Avoid_: Desired concentration, prescribed strength

**Recipe**:
A whole number of scoops plus a volume of water rounded to the nearest 5 mL, which together make up a bottle.
_Avoid_: Mix, preparation

**Actual volume**:
The total volume a recipe really produces: the water plus the displacement of all its powder.
_Avoid_: Final volume

**Actual concentration**:
The Concentration a recipe really produces, which may differ from the Target concentration because of rounding.

**Fewer-scoops suggestion**:
An alternative recipe using one fewer scoop, offered only when its Actual volume is closer to the Target volume and within 10 mL of it.
_Avoid_: Alternative recipe, option

### Formula properties

**Scoop**:
One level measure of powder using the scoop supplied in that formula's tin.

**Grams per scoop**:
The mass of powder in one scoop, read or derived from the formula's label.
_Avoid_: Scoop weight, scoop size

**kcal per scoop**:
The energy in one scoop of powder, derived from the formula's label.

**Reconstitution statement**:
The label's statement of how much prepared formula a known amount of powder and water makes, such as "1 scoop + 50 mL water makes approximately 55 mL".
_Avoid_: Mixing ratio, yield statement

**Displacement**:
The volume in mL that one gram of powder adds to the prepared formula beyond the water it is mixed into, in mL per g.
Displacement per scoop is grams per scoop × displacement. It is used in working, but it isn't an input.
_Avoid_: Powder volume, displacement volume

**Total powder**:
The mass of powder in a recipe in g: its scoops × grams per scoop.
