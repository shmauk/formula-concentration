Answer key

Making up infant formula at a Target concentration

Full working for every exercise. Numbers may differ in the last decimal place if you rounded at a different point, but the recipe should match.

1 Reading a label

Work out the displacement and kcal per scoop of Formula A.

Formula A: the label says

  • Energy: 68 kcal per 100 mL
  • Reconstitution statement: 2 scoops + 60 mL water makes approximately 67 mL
  • Grams per scoop: 1 scoop = 4.6 g
StepWorkingAnswer
A1 Scoops in the statement Given on the label 2 scoops
A2 Displacement (mL per g) (67 − 60) ÷ (2 × 4.6) 0.761 mL per g
A3 kcal per scoop 68 ÷ 100 × (67 ÷ 2) 22.8 kcal

3 Building a recipe

Formula C has 21.5 kcal per scoop, 4.5 g per scoop and a displacement of 0.711 mL per g. Make 180 mL at 20 kcal/30 mL.

StepWorkingAnswer
1 kcal/mL 20 ÷ 30 0.667
2 kcal needed 180 × 0.667 120 kcal
3 Scoops 120 ÷ 21.5 = 5.58, round up 6 scoops
4 Actual kcal 6 × 21.5 129.0 kcal
5 Total powder (g) 6 × 4.5 27.0 g
6 Total displacement (mL) 27.0 × 0.711 19.2 mL
7 Water (mL) 129.0 ÷ 0.667 − 19.2 = 174.3, round up to 5 mL 175 mL
8 Actual volume (mL) 175 + 19.2 194.2 mL
9 Actual concentration (kcal/30 mL) 129.0 ÷ 194.2 × 30 19.93 kcal/30 mL

Recipe: 6 scoops + 175 mL water

Makes: 194.2 mL at 19.93 kcal/30 mL

0.5 kcal/30 mL or more under target? No

8 Explain

  1. Why isn't the water simply the Target volume?

    The powder takes up room too. Every gram of it adds its displacement to the volume, so water equal to the Target volume would make more than the Target volume, at a lower concentration than intended.

  2. Why are scoops rounded up rather than to the nearest whole scoop?

    You can only use whole scoops, and rounding up makes sure there is at least enough energy. The water is then sized to dilute that energy back to the Target concentration. Rounding down would leave too little energy for the Target volume.

  3. A recipe's Actual concentration comes out 0.5 kcal/30 mL or more below the Target concentration. What should you do?

    Double-check the inputs and the working before using it. A large drop usually means a small Target volume with a large scoop, where one scoop is a big step; confirm the result is acceptable for the patient.
