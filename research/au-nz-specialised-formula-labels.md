# AU/NZ specialised and preterm formula labels: does the page 1 method work?

Research for [Research AU/NZ labels for specialised and preterm formulas](https://github.com/shmauk/formula-concentration/issues/15), part of the map [Formula concentration website](https://github.com/shmauk/formula-concentration/issues/1). Accessed **2026-09-30**. Values come from manufacturer-owned pages, manufacturer data sheets and pack images, and AU hospital or professional-body documents. **Nothing here has been checked against a physical tin.** Check every value against a tin before using it in a worked example or test fixture.

The page 1 method (from [Decide the method for getting Grams per scoop, Kcal per scoop and Displacement from AU/NZ labels](https://github.com/shmauk/formula-concentration/issues/8)):

- Displacement = (prepared volume − water) ÷ Scoops
- Kcal per scoop = kcal per 100 mL × (prepared volume ÷ Scoops) ÷ 100
- Scoops = grams ÷ Grams per scoop, when the label gives grams

It needs three things from the label: energy per 100 mL, a Reconstitution statement that includes a **prepared volume**, and Grams per scoop when the statement is in grams.

## Headline findings

1. **The method works on most of the specialised powders clinicians concentrate**: Aptamil Gold+ Pepti-Junior, Aptamil AllerPro Syneo 1, Aptamil Gold+ Reflux and NAN EXPERTpro Reflux all print a usable Reconstitution statement. Almost all come out at about **22 kcal and 3.3 mL per Scoop** (30 mL water per scoop of 4.3–4.8 g). Aptamil Gold+ Reflux is the exception (50 mL per 7.6 g scoop, about 37.5 kcal and 6 mL per Scoop).
2. **Neocate (Gold, LCP, Syneo Infant) gives no prepared volume.** Instead it gives a **% w/v** ("one level scoop … to 30 ml of water to yield the recommended feed concentration of 14.4 %w/v"). Prepared volume = grams ÷ (w/v ÷ 100). This works but is **one step the page 1 method does not teach**.
3. **Three formulas don't give a prepared volume at all**, so Displacement can't be derived from the label:
   - **Alula Reflux** and **Alula Allergy** (Sanulac) print their nutrition panel per "13.5g + 90mL water" (a serving, not 100 mL), in **kJ only**. Kcal per scoop can be derived; Displacement can't.
   - **Alfamino** (Nestlé) gives **per 100 g and per 100 mL** and "Average Scoop Size = 4.4g", but the water per scoop is only on the can's feeding table, which isn't online. Kcal per scoop comes from per 100 g × Grams per scoop (not the page 1 route).
4. **Preterm: there is no AU/NZ preterm or post-discharge powder with a label to use.**
   - **Aptamil Gold+ Preterm** is a **70 mL ready-to-feed liquid** only (80 kcal/100 mL), for hospital use.
   - **PreNAN** is used in AU neonatal units (it appears in SA Health's comparison tables), but there is **no AU Nestlé product page or label** for it. Nestlé's AU HCP site doesn't list it.
   - **S-26/Alula Gold Premgro** (post-discharge powder) only turns up on retailer listings. It isn't in Sanulac's current range and appears discontinued.
   - Preterm infants on breast milk are fortified with **human milk fortifier sachets dosed in grams per mL of milk**, not with the scoop method.
5. **Not current or not found in AU:**
   - **Alfaré** and **Althéra** aren't in ASCIA's June 2026 list of AU/NZ specialised formulas. Nestlé's AU eHF is now **Extensive HA**, and its online data sheet has no energy, scoop or feeding-table values.
   - **EleCare / EleCare LCP** (Abbott) is PBS-listed per ASCIA, but Abbott's AU sites have no EleCare page. The US label (different scoop and mixing) must not be used for AU.

## Per-product detail and sources

All accessed 2026-09-30.

### Aptamil Gold+ Pepti-Junior (Nutricia), 450 g: extensively hydrolysed

- HCP page: https://nutricia.com.au/hcp/product/aptamil-gold-pepti-junior/ (NZ: https://nutricia.co.nz/paediatrics/product/aptamil-gold-pepti-junior/)
- "Per 100mL: 277 kJ / 66 kcal". "Always add one level scoop of powder for each 30mL of water. 1 scoop = 4.3g of powder. 1 scoop of powder added to 30mL of water yields approximately 33.3 mL of formula."
- Feeding guide runs from 60 mL water + 2 scoops (up to 2 weeks) to 240 mL + 8 scoops.
- "Available on PBS". ASCIA lists it as "PBS and PSA listed (prescription required)".
- Also used in AU NICUs as a **fortifier for breast milk, dosed in grams per mL** (Royal Hospital for Women LOP, below). That's outside the scoop method.
- Cross-check: 4.3 g per 33.3 mL = 12.9 g/100 mL, which gives 511 kcal/100 g, in line with other formulas.

### Aptamil AllerPro Syneo 1 (Nutricia): extensively hydrolysed

- HCP page: https://nutricia.com.au/hcp/product/paediatric/aptamil/aptamil-allerpro-syneo-1/
- "Per 100 mL: 275 kJ / 66 kcal". "1 scoop = 4.6g of powder". "1 scoop of powder added to 30mL of water yields approximately 33.3 mL of formula".
- "Available in pharmacy and selected supermarkets without a script in Australia. Available on prescription in New Zealand, fully PHARMAC funded".
- **Inconsistency to flag:** Pepti-Junior (4.3 g) and AllerPro (4.6 g) both claim the same 33.3 mL yield from 30 mL water. So the "33.3 mL" looks like a standard figure (30 mL + 10%) rather than a measured one. Treat Displacement as approximate, the same caveat as Nutricia's "approximately 55 mL" in the standard-formula research.

### Aptamil Gold+ Reflux (Nutricia): anti-reflux, thickened

- HCP page: https://nutricia.com.au/hcp/product/paediatric/aptamil/aptamil-gold-reflux/
- "Per 100mL: 281 kJ / 67 kcal". "1 scoop = 7.6g of powder". "1 scoop of powder added to 50mL of water yields approximately 56 mL of formula".
- "Leave to stand for up to 7 minutes to allow it to thicken". "The formula will continue to thicken after preparation and will remain thick in the stomach".
- Unlike most specialised formulas, this one uses the Nutricia standard-formula convention of 50 mL water per large scoop, so one Scoop is about 37.5 kcal. Rounding to whole Scoops costs more precision here.

- Product page: https://www.nestlefamilynes.com.au/nan-infant-formulas/nan-expertpro-reflux
- Nutrition Information Panel image: https://www.nestlefamilynes.com.au/sites/default/files/styles/content_media_product_tablet_retina/public/content_image/11_9.png
- Feeding table image: https://www.nestlefamilynes.com.au/sites/default/files/styles/content_media_product_tablet_retina/public/content_image/8_11.png
- NIP header "Average amount per 100mL made up formula†": Energy 281 kJ / 67 Cal. Footer: "Average scoop weight = 4.5g" and "†100mL = 13.6g of powder + 90mL of water".
- Feeding table: 90 mL + 3 scoops, 120 + 4, 150 + 5, 180 + 6, 210 + 7 (30 mL water per scoop).

## Implications for the product

1. The page 1 method covers the common eHF and anti-reflux powders unchanged. The label helper's existing "Scoops | grams" switch handles NAN EXPERTpro Reflux, but the **grams route can give a decimal Scoop count** (3.02), so it must not round Scoops to a whole number.
2. **Neocate needs a third form of Reconstitution statement: "% w/v" (g per 100 mL prepared).** Options: teach the one-line conversion (prepared volume = grams ÷ (w/v ÷ 100)), or add "g per 100 mL" as a third choice in the helper. Otherwise Neocate users must derive the 33.3 mL themselves.
3. **Labels with "X g + Y mL water" and no volume** (Alula Reflux and Allergy), or **per 100 g and a feeding table only** (Alfamino), can't give Displacement from the label. Page 1 should say so, and point users to entering Displacement measured on a real tin or from the manufacturer's careline. The ~3.3 mL per 30 mL scoop seen across other specialised formulas is a plausible default, but it isn't from these labels.
4. Most specialised formulas use a small scoop (about 22 kcal per Scoop), so whole-Scoop rounding is finer than with Aptamil/Karicare standard formulas (about 38 kcal).
5. **Preterm formulas aren't a label-method case in AU/NZ**: they're ready-to-feed liquids or hospital products with no AU label online, and breast-milk fortification is dosed by weight. Page 1 should state that the method doesn't apply to them.
6. Anti-reflux formulas thicken on standing. Concentrating them changes thickness and flow, which is a clinical caveat to show alongside any worked example with them.
